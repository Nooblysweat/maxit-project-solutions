A few helpful details

Before we get printing.

Can I order from outside Johannesburg?

Yes. Online orders are welcome, and shipping and courier services are available nationwide at an additional fee. Include your town in your enquiry so delivery can be discussed.

What should I include in my quote request?

Include the product/service, dimensions, quantity, material or finish, installation requirements and deadline. Tell us if you need design assistance, and email any high-resolution print-ready artwork.

Can you help if I don’t have artwork?

Ask us about design support. Let us know what you have — a logo, text or a rough idea — so we can discuss the work needed and include it in your quote.

How should I send artwork, and will I see a proof?

Email original high-resolution files to info@maxitprints.co.za with your name and project details. Artwork sent as WhatsApp images may lose quality. We check and confirm the final design with you for approval before printing.

How much will my order cost, and how long will it take?

Pricing and timing depend on the product, quantity, artwork and finishing. Request a quote so we can confirm the options and availability for your specific project.
